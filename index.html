The workshop

Built from what was already there

The GateKeeper is a one-person workshop with a grinder, a welder and an unreasonable fondness for other people's junk. Machines get taken apart. Spiders get built out of the parts. Nothing is wasted, because nothing here started life as art in the first place.

  • Steel scrap
  • Copper pipe
  • Brass fittings
  • Chain
  • Gears
  • Cutlery
  • Spanners
  • Salvaged loom parts

Every piece is welded by hand, finished with heat and patina, and built to be heavy enough that nobody accidentally walks off with it.

How a piece happens

From scrapyard to shelf

  1. Hunt

    It starts with a shape that shouldn't work — a grinder, a loom, a box of bent forks.

  2. Sketch

    Rough lines on scrap paper until the pile of parts agrees with the drawing.

  3. Cut & weld

    Grinders, sparks, tack welds. The piece gets its skeleton before it gets its manners.

  4. Finish

    Heat, patina, wax. Copper goes warm, steel goes dark, and the whole thing stops looking like scrap.
